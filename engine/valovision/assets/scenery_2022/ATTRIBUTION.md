# Scenery

World views cropped from real VALORANT player screenshots (January–February 2022) in
[deepsidh9/Live-Valorant-Overlay](https://github.com/deepsidh9/Live-Valorant-Overlay) (MIT licence).
The HUD is cropped away and the crosshair painted out; built by `tools/make_scenery.py`.
VALORANT imagery is © Riot Games, used here for a non-commercial technical demonstration.
